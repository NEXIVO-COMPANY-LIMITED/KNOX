# Business Requirements

## 1. Executive Summary
The KNOX Mobile Device Management (MDM) solution is a dedicated enterprise infrastructure designed to transform Samsung mobile devices into secured corporate assets. This system provides a hardware-backed management layer, ensuring that all organization-issued devices adhere to strict security, operational, and financial compliance standards.

## 2. Strategic Objectives
*   **Asset Protection:** Implement hardware-level locks to prevent unauthorized use or theft of corporate property.
*   **Operational Compliance:** Ensure all devices maintain a predefined security posture, including mandatory encryption and restricted connectivity.
*   **Automated Provisioning:** Reduce IT overhead through zero-touch deployment via Samsung Knox Mobile Enrollment (KME).
*   **Security Hardening:** Utilize the Samsung Knox platform to provide protections superior to standard Android Enterprise implementations.

## 3. Organizational Requirements
### 3.1 Hardware Standards
*   Exclusive use of Samsung Galaxy enterprise-grade devices.
*   Requirement for Knox 3.x+ hardware support for firmware-level security.

### 3.2 Security Posture
*   Mandatory device encryption.
*   Prohibition of unauthorized firmware modifications (Rooting).
*   Restricted access to external interfaces (USB, Bluetooth) based on security zones.

### 3.3 Lifecycle Management
*   Atomic device registration linked to organizational asset IDs.
*   Real-time telemetry for health monitoring and policy enforcement.
*   Automated offboarding upon asset retirement or contract completion.

## 4. Key Stakeholders
*   **IT Administration:** Responsible for policy definition and fleet monitoring.
*   **Security Operations:** Oversees audit logs and threat response.
*   **Asset Management:** Manages device distribution and inventory lifecycle.
